# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful, and useful resource for people preparing for Product Manager interviews and building their next career opportunity.

We are committed to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, role, location, or choice of AI provider.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Give feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Welcome different career paths, product perspectives, and interview approaches.
- Protect privacy by never posting someone else's CV, résumé, personal data, or interview content without permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate exposure of private information, unwanted commercial promotion, and any conduct that would make a reasonable participant feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

To report a concern, contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub rather than posting sensitive details publicly in an issue or discussion.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is adapted for this project from the principles of the Contributor Covenant. It is intended to support a respectful open-source community.
