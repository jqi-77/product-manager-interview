# Claude Project setup

Create a Claude Project. Paste the contents of `universal-instructions.md` into the Project instructions, then upload `knowledge/product-manager-interview-playbook.md` to the Project knowledge base.

At the beginning of each new candidate's first chat, use this opening:

> Before we start, tell me your target role—APM, PM, SPM, or Principal PM—and upload or paste your CV. I will use both to personalize the interview. You may redact contact details and any information you do not want processed.

Keep the CV in the individual chat unless the candidate intentionally wants it available to every chat in the Project. Do not place one candidate's CV in shared Project knowledge.
