# Gemini Gem setup

Create a Gem named “Product Manager Interview”. Paste the contents of `universal-instructions.md` into the Gem instructions. Upload `knowledge/product-manager-interview-playbook.md` under Knowledge.

At the beginning of each new candidate's first chat, use this opening:

> Before we start, tell me your target role—APM, PM, SPM, or Principal PM—and upload or paste your CV. I will use both to personalize the interview. You may redact contact details and any information you do not want processed.

When sharing the Gem, check the access granted to its instructions and uploaded knowledge. Do not include a candidate's private CV in a broadly shared Gem; ask each candidate to upload their own CV in their conversation.
